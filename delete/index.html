Delete your account and data

LemonVanilla · Last updated 2026-10-19

This page is for LemonVanilla's apps: Lattice, Roundtuit, Echo and Roots. You can ask for your account and its data to be deleted here, even if the app is no longer on your phone.

How to ask

Email bluehourapps@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with. Say which app. We reply when it's done, within 7 days.

We only act on requests from the account's own email address, so nobody can delete someone else's account.

Before you ask

If you have a Sync Monthly or Sync Yearly plan, cancel it in Google Play first (Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). Deleting your account doesn't cancel it.

What gets deleted

  • Your account for that app.
  • Everything that app keeps for you on our servers.

Each app's account is separate. Deleting your account in one app doesn't touch your data in the others. To delete more than one, name each app.

What isn't deleted

  • Backups the app made to your own Google Drive. They're in your Drive, and only you can delete them.
  • What's stored on your phone. Uninstall the app, or use Wipe all data in its settings.

How long we keep data

When a Sync plan ends, your data moves to your phone and we keep the server copy through the end of the next full calendar month, in case you come back. Then it's deleted. A deletion request removes it straight away.
