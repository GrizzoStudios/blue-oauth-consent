Roundtuit: Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-05

Roundtuit is a private to-do list app used by its owner and their household. It is not offered to the public.

What it collects

  • From Google Sign-In: your name and email address, used only to sign you in and keep your list yours.
  • The tasks you enter: what the task is, who asked for it and when, its category, how often it comes up, its status, notes, cost and time spent.
  • Your preferences (display name, theme, which fields show), kept on your phone.

Where it is stored

Tasks are stored in the owner's private database, where only your own signed-in account can read or change your rows. A copy is kept on your phone so the list shows offline. The owner's personal AI assistant can read the owner's tasks and, only with the owner's approval each time, add or update them.

What it doesn't do

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for any other purpose. No ads, analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

Delete a task in the app by swiping it left. To have everything removed, contact the developer at the email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Roundtuit. To remove Roundtuit's access to your Google account: Google Account → Security → Third-party access → Roundtuit → Remove access.

Contact

Use the developer email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Roundtuit.
