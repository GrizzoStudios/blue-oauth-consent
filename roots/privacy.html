Roots: Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-27

Roots is a private garden planner for one household's own use. It is not offered to the public.

What Google Sign-In shares

  • The Google account's name and email address, used only to identify the account that owns the garden.
  • If you turn on Google Drive Backup: access to the files Roots itself makes in your Google Drive, and nothing else there. Roots cannot see your other Drive files, Gmail or contacts.

What Roots keeps

  • Your garden: its map and beds, your plants and any plants you add to the plant list, your journal, care reminders, harvests and seasons, garden score history, your settings, and the optional profile you fill in (display name, gender, birth month and year). It is stored on your phone, and there is no sign-in until you pick a paid plan.
  • If you use Sync, the same garden is kept in a private cloud database, where row-level security limits each row to its owner's account, so it follows you to another phone. Photos stay on the phone they were taken on; only the entries sync.
  • If you use Google Drive Backup, a copy of your garden is saved to a folder Roots makes in your own Google Drive.
  • Feedback you send from the app, with the app version, plan, phone model and Android version, and a reply email only if you give one.

Who else is involved

  • For the forecast and frost and heat alerts, the garden's latitude and longitude are sent to the US National Weather Service and to Open-Meteo. Roots never reads the phone's location: the garden's place comes from the ZIP code or place you type in.
  • Growing advice works only if you add your own key for Anthropic, ChatGPT or Gemini. Then the plant's name, the garden's place and zone, and the beds' sizes, sun and watering go to the service you chose, under its own privacy policy.
  • If the account owner connects their own AI assistant through the developer's connector, it reads that owner's synced garden as that owner, under the same row-level security. No one else's.
  • If paid plans are offered, purchases are handled by Google Play. Roots never sees card details.

What it doesn't do

Nothing is sold or used for advertising. There are no ads, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no location tracking.

Your choices

  • Everything can be exported or deleted in the app, under Settings → Data, including deleting the account. You can also ask for deletion on the account and data deletion page.
  • To remove Roots' access to your Google account: Google Account → Security → Third-party access → Roots → Remove access.
