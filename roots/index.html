Roots

A garden planner for Android

Roots is a private garden planner built by an individual developer for one household's own use. It keeps the garden's map and beds, the plants in them, a garden journal, care reminders, harvests and seasons, with local weather and frost alerts. It is not offered to the public and is not listed in any app store.

Roots uses Google Sign-In only to identify the account that owns the garden, and Google Drive only for backups it makes itself.
