Echo

A podcast player for Android

Echo is a private podcast player built by an individual developer for one household's own use. It plays podcasts from their public RSS feeds, keeps a Queue, and remembers where each episode left off. It is not offered to the public and is not listed in any app store.

Echo uses Google Sign-In only to identify the account that owns the library, and Google Drive only for backups it makes itself.
