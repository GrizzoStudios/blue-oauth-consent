Lattice

A personal health tracker for Android

Lattice is a private, non-commercial app built by an individual developer for one household's own use. It keeps a personal record of things like workouts, food, weight, sleep and lab results. It is not offered to the public, sold, or listed in any app store.

Lattice uses Google Sign-In only to identify the account that owns the data. It asks Google for nothing beyond the account's name and email address.
