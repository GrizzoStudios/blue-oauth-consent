Lattice: Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-08

Lattice is a private, non-commercial health tracker for Android, used by one household. It is not offered to anyone else. The complete privacy policy is inside the app, under Settings; this page summarizes it.

What Google Sign-In shares

  • The Google account's name and email address, used only to create and identify the account that owns the data.
  • Lattice asks Google for no other permission. It cannot see the account's Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else.

What Lattice keeps

  • What the user logs or chooses to connect (for example workouts, food, weight, sleep and lab results), on the phone and in a private cloud database where row-level security limits each row to its owner's account.
  • Data from a health service only when the user connects it in the app, and only what that connection lists.

What it doesn't do

Nothing is sold or used for advertising. There are no ads, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no location tracking.

Your choices

  • Everything can be viewed, exported or deleted in the app, under Settings, including deleting the account.
  • To remove Lattice's access to the Google account: Google Account → Security → Third-party access → Lattice → Remove access.

Contact

Use the developer email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Lattice, or the contact in the app's own privacy policy.
