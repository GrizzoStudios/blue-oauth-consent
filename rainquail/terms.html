Rainquail: Terms of Use

Last updated 2026-10-27

Rainquail is a private sprinkler system manager for one household's own use. It is not offered to the public.

Your sprinklers

Rainquail can start and stop watering and change the programs stored on your Rain Bird controller. The controller runs them on its own, and Rainquail cannot see a leak, a broken head or a valve that sticks. Keep an eye on your system, and follow your city's watering rules: the app's restriction warnings are a reminder, not a legal check.

Watering advice

Watering rates, catch-cup results and weather-based suggestions are general guidance for a home yard, not professional advice. The forecast comes from an outside service and can be wrong. Rainquail never changes a schedule on its own.

Your data

Your yard stays yours. How it is handled is described in the privacy policy.

No warranty

Rainquail is provided as is, without any warranty. Rain Bird is a trademark of its owner; Rainquail is not made or endorsed by Rain Bird.

Age

Rainquail is intended for people 13 and older.
